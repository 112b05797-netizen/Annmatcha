Our Story

Slow down in the calming aroma of matcha.

A Journey of Calm

Ann Matcha began as a quiet space created for people who love the gentle aroma of matcha. Every drink and dessert is made with careful balance—flavor, comfort, and warmth.

Pet-Friendly Philosophy

The café grew with our store cat Ninety-Nine. We hope every visitor—human or furry friend—feels welcomed, relaxed, and at ease.

Milestones

2021 – The Beginning

Ann Matcha started as a small passion project inspired by peaceful tea moments.

2023 – Store Cat Arrives

Ninety-Nine joined the family and became the heart of the café.

2025 – Expanding Creativity

We introduced merch, card games, and new matcha dessert editions.
